ItemTypeWhat it is
AntennaCircuitTx and Rx antennas and the chip-to-antenna transition; its loss adds straight to the noise figure and subtracts from Tx power.
PACircuitPower amplifier: drives the chirp into the Tx antenna through a transformer balun.
LNACircuitLow-noise amplifier: first gain stage of the receiver; its noise and gain set the receiver noise figure.
Mixer + TIACircuitPassive double-balanced mixer with a transimpedance amplifier: multiplies the echo by the LO to give the IF beat signal.
IF filter / HPFCircuitHigh-pass and anti-alias filters in the IF chain: suppress close-in leakage and set the IF band before the ADC.
VGA / PGACircuitProgrammable IF gain amplifier with switched feedback resistors; sets the signal level for the ADC.
ADCCircuitConverts the IF beat signal to digital; also used by BIST to measure on-chip detectors.
DFECircuitDigital front end: decimation, filtering and data formatting before the radar processing.
Radar DSP (FFT)CircuitRange and Doppler FFTs, CFAR detection and angle estimation.
Phase rotatorCircuitSets the phase of each Tx channel for beam steering and MIMO coding; built from I/Q vector summing.
Rotator DACCircuitCurrent DACs that weight the I and Q vectors of the phase rotator; their INL/DNL and bow set phase accuracy.
Power detectorCircuitDiode or square-law detector on the Tx output coupler; its DC output tells the chip the Tx power.
PFD + charge pumpCircuitPhase-frequency detector and charge pump: compare reference and divided VCO and pump current into the loop filter.
Loop filterCircuitRC network that turns charge-pump current into the VCO tuning voltage and sets loop bandwidth and stability.
VCOCircuitLC-tank voltage-controlled oscillator (about 19–20 GHz, then ×4) with switched-capacitor sub-bands.
Divider / ΣΔCircuitMulti-modulus divider driven by a sigma-delta modulator for fractional-N division.
Ramp generatorCircuitDigital chirp generator that steps the fractional divide ratio to sweep the frequency.
×4 multiplierCircuitFrequency multiplier from the VCO band to 76–81 GHz.
LO distributionCircuitBuffers and lines that carry the 77 GHz LO to every Tx rotator and Rx mixer.
LDOCircuitLow-dropout regulators that give each block a clean, local supply.
BandgapCircuitTemperature-stable voltage reference for LDOs, monitors, ADC and bias currents.
UV / OV monitorCircuitWindow comparators that flag under- and over-voltage on each supply for functional safety.
POR & sequencingCircuitPower-on reset and the order in which supplies, references and clocks come up.
ESD diodesCircuitProtection diodes on every pin; open/short test forward-biases them to check continuity.
Scan chainsCircuitFlip-flops linked into shift registers so the digital logic can be controlled and observed by the tester.
Memory BISTCircuitOn-chip March-algorithm tester for the radar data memories.
Logic BISTCircuitPseudo-random self-test of the logic, also run in the field at key-on.
JTAG / IJTAGCircuitTest access port and instrument network that reach scan, BIST and trim registers.
Test mux / ATBCircuitAnalog test muxes and the analog test bus: connect each sub-block node to the aux ADC, main ADC or a test pin.
Loopback switchesCircuitRF SP4T switches and splitter that choose which Tx feeds which Rx in the loopback.
Digital test muxesCircuitScan, clock, reset, bypass and pin-sharing muxes that test mode controls.
BIST controllerCircuitOn-chip sequencer that sets up loopback paths, runs measurements and compares results with limits.
Noise figureParameterHow much noise the receiver adds; set mostly by input loss and the LNA (Friis).
Rx gainParameterTotal receive-chain gain from antenna to ADC; high LNA gain hides later noise.
IIP3 / P1dBParameterLinearity of the receive chain; late stages (mixer, VGA) usually limit it.
Tx output powerParameterPower at the Tx ball, typically 10–14 dBm per channel.
PAEParameterPower-added efficiency of the PA; sets heat and supply current.
Phase noiseParameterNoise on the LO phase; limits detection of weak targets next to strong ones.
Chirp linearityParameterHow straight the frequency ramp is; nonlinearity smears range peaks.
Lock time / lock detectParameterHow fast the PLL settles and how the chip knows it is locked.
KVCO / tuning rangeParameterVCO gain (MHz/V) and the frequency span of each sub-band.
I/Q imbalanceParameterGain and phase mismatch between I and Q paths; creates image (ghost) targets.
ADC SNR / ENOBParameterEffective resolution of the ADC; sets the dynamic range at the digital side.
DAC INL / DNLParameterLinearity of the rotator DACs; bow (2nd-order INL) becomes phase and amplitude error.
PSRRParameterHow well a regulator or circuit rejects supply noise; poor PSRR shows up as spurs.
Dropout / load regulationParameterMinimum headroom across the LDO and its output change with load current.
VthParameterTransistor threshold voltage; shifts speed, bias points and leakage.
fT / fmaxParameterTransistor cut-off and maximum oscillation frequencies; set the headroom at 77 GHz.
gm / IdsatParameterTransconductance and saturation current: the drive strength of the transistor.
Ioff / leakageParameterOff-state transistor current; sets standby and IDDQ baseline.
Breakdown voltageParameterJunction, oxide and drain breakdown limits; cap PA swing and stress-test levels.
Sheet R / matchingParameterResistor and capacitor values and their matching; set gain steps, filter corners and trim range.
Contact resistanceParameterProbe or socket contact resistance; drifts as needles wear and falsely fails parametric tests.
Max range / SNRParameterDetection range from the radar equation: Tx power, antenna gain, NF and integration time.
Range resolutionParameterc / 2B: set by the chirp bandwidth.
CpkParameterProcess capability: distance from the mean to the nearest limit in units of 3σ.
Open / short testTestForces current into each pin through its ESD diode to find opens and shorts before any other test.
Walking-ZTestTri-states pins one at a time to find pin-to-pin shorts and leakage.
IDDQTestQuiescent supply current in several scan states; a raised value reveals bridges and gate-oxide defects.
At-speed testTestTransition and path-delay scan patterns at the functional clock to catch slow paths.
HVST / burn-inTestHigh-voltage stress and burn-in accelerate weak oxide and metal defects to failure before shipment.
VLV / VminTestVery-low-voltage test: runs logic and memory below nominal supply to expose marginal cells and resistive defects.
RF loopbackTestRoutes the Tx signal back into the Rx on-chip so the whole RF path is tested without mmWave instruments.
On-chip measurementTestDetectors, counters and the ADC measure power, frequency, gain and phase inside the chip (BISM).
Trim & calibrationTestMeasured errors are written back as trim codes: bandgap, LDO, VCO band, I/Q, phase, gain.
PCM / WATTestScribe-line test structures measured on every wafer before sort: Vth, Idsat, leakage, sheet R, breakdown.
SPCTestControl charts and run rules that keep each WAT parameter in control.
PAT / GDBNTestOutlier screens: part-average testing and good-die-in-bad-neighbourhood removal for zero-defect automotive.
AEC-Q100TestAutomotive qualification: HTOL, temperature cycling, ESD, latch-up and more.
Production test flowTestThe order of test insertions from wafer sort to final test and system test.
Fail binsTestWhere each failing die is sorted; bin data drives yield analysis.
Corners & mismatchFab & packageProcess corners (SS/FF/SF/FS) and local mismatch that designs must survive.
Wafer map signatureFab & packageSpatial patterns of failing die that point to a process tool or setting.
CMPFab & packageChemical-mechanical polishing: centre/edge rings from pad wear, slurry or pressure zones.
Reticle / lithoFab & packageExposure and reticle issues that repeat in every shot on the wafer.
Fan-out (eWLB)Fab & packageFan-out wafer-level package common for 77 GHz radar: low-loss RDL, no bond wires.
Antenna in packageFab & packageAntennas built into the package, removing the PCB RF launch.
RDLFab & packageRedistribution layers that route die pads to balls and carry the mmWave transitions.
Bump / ballFab & packageSolder bumps and balls: pitch, height and under-bump metallurgy.
Solder fatigueFab & packageThermal cycling cracks the solder joints; the leading package failure in automotive.
Temperature cyclingFab & packageCycling −40/+125 °C (or −55/+150 °C) accelerates fatigue and delamination.
Anti-alias filterCircuitLow-pass filter before the ADC that stops signals near fs from folding into the band; relaxed with a CT-ΣΔ ADC.
Decimation filterCircuitCIC and half-band FIR stages that turn the ΣΔ bit stream into 16-bit samples at 2× the IF bandwidth.
Package qualificationTestAEC-Q100 stress tests of the packaged chip: preconditioning, TC, HAST/THB, autoclave, HTSL, HTOL, with read-points and pass criteria.
Cross-section / DPATestPhysical analysis after stress: CSAM, X-ray, cross-sections, SEM/EDX and shear tests.
Assembly flowFab & packageBumping, dicing, fan-out molding, RDL build-up, ball attach and singulation.
Die shift / warpageFab & packageDie movement during molding and package bending at reflow; cause RDL misalignment and open joints.
Bench bring-upTestStep-by-step first-silicon test on the evaluation board, from continuity to system test.
OTA / chamber testTestOver-the-air measurement in an anechoic chamber with reflectors and target simulators.
Radome / bumperFab & packagePlastic cover in front of the antennas; its thickness and paint set loss and reflections.
Module EOL calibrationTestAntenna gain and phase calibration and boresight alignment of the finished module.
Junction temperatureParameterDie temperature set by power and the thermal path; drives RF performance and aging.
Power delivery / PICircuitRegulators, planes and decoupling; supply noise here becomes LO spurs.
EMC testsTestCISPR 25 emissions and ISO 11452 immunity tests of the module.
EM / post-layout simulationTestElectromagnetic models and parasitic extraction that predict mmWave performance before tape-out.
Test costParameterCost per good die from tester rate, test time, sites and yield.
eFuse / OTPCircuitOne-time-programmable bits (poly eFuse, antifuse) that store trim codes, die ID and repair for life.
Shadow registersCircuitRegisters loaded from the fuses at boot; they drive the trim DACs and allow soft trim during test.
ACCSystemAdaptive cruise control: keeps a time gap to the car ahead using the front long-range radar.
AEBSystemAutomatic emergency braking for cars, pedestrians and cyclists, from time-to-collision.
BSD / LCASystemBlind-spot detection and lane-change assist from the rear corner radars.
RCTA / RCTBSystemRear cross-traffic alert and braking when reversing.
4D imaging radarSystemHigh-channel radar that also measures elevation, for L2+ and L3 driving.
Saturation detectorCircuitWindow comparators, peak detectors and ADC over-range flags that report clipping in each receive stage.
Clock jitterParameterTiming noise of the ADC clock; limits SNR at high IF frequencies.
Radar cube memoryCircuitOn-chip SRAM holding range × chirp × channel data for the Doppler and angle processing.
CFAR detectionSystemAdaptive threshold that finds targets at a constant false-alarm rate.
Angle estimationSystemDirection of arrival over the virtual array: FFT beamforming or super-resolution.
Data interfaceCircuitCSI-2, automotive Ethernet, SPI or CAN-FD link to the host.
FTTIParameterFault-tolerant time interval: detection and reaction must finish within it.
SPFM / LFM / PMHFParameterISO 26262 hardware metrics that the FMEDA must meet for the ASIL.
Key-on testsTestStart-up self-tests of logic, memories and the monitors themselves, for latent faults.
ESD power clampCircuitRC-triggered large NMOS between supply rails that carries the ESD current.
HBM / CDMTestESD qualification stresses: human-body and charged-device models.
Latch-upFab & packageParasitic thyristor turning on and shorting the supplies.
HCIFab & packageHot-carrier injection: drain-side damage that lowers gain and output power over life.
NBTI / PBTIFab & packageThreshold shift under gate bias at high temperature; slows logic and shifts analog bias.
TDDBFab & packageTime-dependent gate-oxide breakdown.
ElectromigrationFab & packageMetal atoms pushed by current density; opens and shorts in supply lines.
HTOL / FITTestHigh-temperature operating life test and the failure rate derived from it.
Mission profileSystem15-year temperature, voltage and on-time profile that sets the aging and FIT budget.
Guard band / test limitsParameterMargin between spec and test limit for measurement error, temperature and aging.
Lab–ATE correlationTestGolden units measured in the lab and on the tester to align production results.
Gage R&RTestRepeatability and reproducibility of the measurement system.
Die yield (D0)ParameterShare of good dies, set by defect density and area plus parametric margin.
DPPM / escapesParameterDefective parts per million that pass test; automotive goal below 1.
SYL / SBLTestStatistical yield and bin limits that hold abnormal lots.
Failure analysis / 8DTestRoot-cause analysis of returns and the 8D corrective-action process.
EIRP / band limitsParameterRegulatory power and band-edge limits in each market.
Radar interferenceSystemOther radars crossing the chirp; raises the noise floor or creates ghosts.
FMCW chirpSystemA linear frequency ramp; the beat frequency between Tx and echo gives range.
Beam steering / MIMOSystemTx phases steer the beam or code the channels so the receiver can separate them (virtual array).
Tx power loopSystemDetector, ADC and digital control that hold the Tx power constant over temperature and ageing.
AGCSystemAutomatic gain control: picks the VGA gain so strong echoes do not clip the ADC.
Supply treeSystemBattery or PMIC rails split into domains and local LDOs.
Functional safetySystemISO 26262 / ASIL: in-field monitors and self-tests that detect faults within the fault-tolerant time.
Digital timingSystemSetup and hold margins of the logic; corners and mismatch can break them.
