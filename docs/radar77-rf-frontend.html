RF front-end circuits: LNA, mixer, PA and VGA

RF · 77 GHzIF

These four circuits carry the radar signal: the PA sends the chirp out, the LNA and mixer bring the weak echo down to IF, and the VGA sets its level for the ADC. At 77 GHz each one works close to the transistor's limits (fT/fmax of a few hundred GHz), so their parameters decide the radar's range and dynamic range. This page shows each circuit, its key parameters with typical values, and how they are measured and protected. The system view is in the signal chain; the transistor behind them is on the PCM / WAT page.
